# Woodstone Festival Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Isola Parallis, the living pulse of the Woodstone Festival.

## Who Can Enter?
- **Initiates:** Newcomers seeking to witness or participate.
- **Tutors:** Guides, mentors, and rhythmkeepers.
- **Witnesses:** Observers and co-witnesses, present in spirit.

## How to Enter

- **Landing:** Visit the homepage. All navigation is keyboard-accessible and screen reader-friendly.
- **Visual/Tactile:** Toggle high-contrast mode for visibility. Visual rhythm can be paused or described in text.
- **Assistance:** Press <kbd>?</kbd> or access Help from any page for instant instructions.
- **No Gatekeepers:** All are welcome. No password or wealth barriers.

## Festival Features

- **Pulse:** Join the Sentimento Rhythm — visually, tactually, or in plain text.
- **Artifacts:** Discover emblems, manifestos, and protocols — all with alt text and screen reader support.
- **Accessibility:** Every element is described, navigable, and pausable.

- Email: woodstone@isola-parallis.world
- Or ask a Tutor via live chat.
